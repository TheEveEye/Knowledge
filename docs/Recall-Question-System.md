## Generation

Questions are planned using the full saved concept definition and statements, plus up to four directly connected neighbours. Every assessable unit carries its own exact evidence, stable material identity and content signature. The units are facts and saved source-backed connections. Source-inferred relationships retain that qualification and must pass source verification; endpoints alone never establish relationship knowledge. Unresolved review notes prevent assessment of the affected unit.

Preparation handles up to six focus units per bank and up to sixteen concurrent workers through the shared account queue. The queue initially permits sixteen active background requests and continues to apply admission spacing, server cooldowns and connection backoff. Each worker generates and verifies sequentially; parallel workers do not bypass the account's limits.

The generator returns questions directly, without a redundant preliminary planning object or mandatory format explanation. It prioritizes one focused fallback per requested unit, then may add two natural integrated questions. Standalone fallbacks for supplied non-focus units are allowed. A failure in one candidate does not discard the whole bank. Every criterion selects one to six evidence IDs belonging to its unit. The application resolves these to original quoted fragments and stores their source references; saved paraphrases are never passed off as verbatim quotes. Several fragments can jointly support a criterion. Optional precision notes are validated separately and omitted if invalid; the verifier can also remove unsupported notes without rejecting a fair question.

The input schema uses format-specific alternatives with required fields and bounded option/pair counts. Controls have one source of truth for options and keys; prompts do not repeat lettered mappings. Scalar-quantity goals prefer a number control or justified approximate slider, while a fair text response remains usable. Retrieval-goal metadata does not invalidate an answerable question. The application never invents a numerical key or tolerance to force a control. Non-numerical frequency such as several times remains text. The question records givenContext separately from the new meaning to retrieve.

Verification returns a keyed review for each candidate with concrete blocking issues: gate, problem, counterexample and repair. An empty issues array accepts the question, avoiding a separate verdict that can contradict its explanation. Checks cover support, answerability, leakage, fair semantic criteria, input validity and unit mapping. Complete criterion propositions do not demand verbatim repetition of givens. Verification requests are chunked by payload size. A removable optional note or stylistic preference is not a blocker.

One initial generation attempt is followed by at most two targeted repairs of missing focused coverage. Repairs receive target-specific failures and compact rejected-question examples, bounded to 12 KB; accepted designs are checkpointed before any repair awaits. Transport retries remain governed by the shared queue. Source review notes or absent evidence are detected before generation and remain blocked for human source review. Formatting/verification failures that exhaust repairs become retryable failed targets, not source issues. The preparation status reports partial completion when applicable.

Verified integrated questions survive a missing fallback: they are retained as alternatives on available targets or in an excluded deferred target until a focused fallback exists. Session eligibility still requires every participant to have an active, valid target; saving a broader question never fabricates readiness or memory credit. Later preparation merges deferred variants into the focused bank. Sources, signatures, reservations and account/task freshness are checked before saving.

## Answer formats

- **Text:** default for explanations, definitions, comparisons and integrated recall; assessed by AI against the saved rubric.
- **Multiple choice:** single select, three to five distinct plausible options and exactly one source-supported key. No forced format quota.
- **Number:** explicit units, finite expected value and nonnegative tolerance. The question must ask for a scalar, not secretly demand a range or explanation.
- **Slider:** a meaningful bounded approximate scale, explicit units and resolution; no submitted default. Precision must agree with tolerance.
- **Ordering:** three to six items in a unique supported sequence. Options are presented independently of the answer key; the learner must explicitly confirm an order.
- **Matching:** two to five one-to-one pairs, with a separate criterion per pair. Answers remain incomplete until every item is matched and each answer is used once.

Choice, scalar and ordering questions assess one criterion. Text can assess several criteria; matching maps each pair independently. Objective answer keys are checked locally, including numerical tolerance, avoiding a grading request for those submissions. Original responses, format definitions and structured selections are persisted before grading and included in opt-in diagnostics. Text answers retain the same AI grading, dispute and recovery controls. Typed follow-ups can use another appropriate format; matching belongs in prepared questions, since a follow-up addresses only one criterion.

## Scheduling and credit

A question is an interaction, not a proficiency unit. Each requested fact or relationship receives its own projected initial/final criterion results, append-only review event and FSRS update. A correct fact can receive credit even when another fact in the same answer was missed. Partial or missing knowledge is not silently marked learned. Connection units require the relationship itself to be demonstrated.

Correct independent production defaults to Good without any self-assessment tap. A collapsed optional control permits Hard (That was difficult) or Easy (Very easy) adjustments only after correct independent recall; tapping the selected adjustment again restores automatic timing. Missing effort remains nil in stored episodes so defaults are distinguishable from actual self-reports. Existing explicit ratings and historical schedules are retained. Incorrect and assisted results continue to follow their assessed evidence. Recognition/cued formats (choice, slider, matching, ordering) receive at most Hard and contribute at most half readiness to the displayed estimate. Recognition does not count as an independent session success. Recognition or prompted follow-ups do not retroactively upgrade the initial production answer. Successfully completing an answer after help can contribute half readiness, but does not manufacture independent recall. Shared Easy ratings are capped at Good for correct components of an overall incomplete answer.

The same AI text-grading request can offer up to twelve additional eligible targets from the requested concepts and their direct saved neighbours. Every offered target gets an explicit quote-or-null decision. Only unsolicited knowledge in the first unassisted production answer can earn this extra credit. Unmentioned facts, connection endpoint names and option-given information never suffice.

Multi-target reviews and volunteered extra credits link to the root review, commit in one transaction and undo together. Before undo, all changed memory states are checked for intervening reviews. Existing history remains intact. Duplicate source-backed relationships share one material. Estimates include each connection in both participating concepts, while that connection has one shared learning record.

## Complete target coverage and independently confirmed extras (v8 / policy v18)

The v7 phone run exposed two different failures: requested parts could be rejected for not restating their entire target, while questions covering only one part could still advance the whole target's memory. It also committed two unsupported extra awards. V8 uses the conservative full-target-card approach rather than introducing new per-facet memory cards or rewriting old events.

- **Part support and collective coverage are distinct.** `criterionSupport` checks whether a criterion is a supported part of its assigned claim; a legitimate part can pass without restating that compound claim. Equivalent fact/connection claims do not compete for one arbitrary catalog ID. Separate `coverage` records assess every binding's entire target and list untested meaningful parts. Only complete coverage with no missing parts is accepted. All existing evidence, input-key, signature and source protections remain.
- **A focused fallback covers the full target.** A body/mind fact needs both parts in one task, possibly separate criterion IDs. Full-list facts need all listed items. Partial questions are not interchangeable alternatives for that card. Matching still has one criterion per pair, even for a compound option, but every bound claim must be covered. Meaningful criteria are not split into isolated adjectives or paired synonyms. Prompt-provided subjects and purposes remain context; distinct target knowledge given away by a prompt cannot be counted as recalled except in deliberate recognition.
- **Proof survives installation, projection and history.** New bindings persist optional `completeCoverage` metadata inside their existing JSON, with no SwiftData columns or schema migration. Installation, new-session eligibility and FSRS updates all require complete proofs and unique complete criterion assignments. Old data decodes with no proof. Old saved sessions may finish with a visible no-schedule-change notice; the new-bank button explains that stored progress remains. Repair preserves target IDs, signatures, memories, due dates, events and prior grades, and skips material reserved in open sessions. Historic unsupported credit is not silently regraded or erased.
- **Repairs retain identities.** The request includes the original keyed criteria, evidence IDs, bound statements and structured input. Coverage/repair schemas restrict criterion unit keys to current focus keys and evidence IDs to eligible units; neighboring units are context. At most one complete focused question per focus key is requested. Enrichment remains separate and optional, with the existing 16 concurrent workers. Luna / Medium preparation preferences are unchanged. Verification chunk budgets include catalog evidence as well as questions.
- **Scope rejection needs source support.** The scope check includes original evidence quotations establishing a different correct answer. A grammatically conceivable wrong response is not an ambiguity. Scope-only issues carry `basis=scope` and block only with a valid, source-supported, uncovered alternative. Genuine source, task and input defects retain their gates.
- **Prompt-relative grading is explicit.** Preparation returns minimal contextual answers, new response meanings and exact `promptGivens` snippets. Only still-visible snippets enter grading after display formatting. Grading resolves pronouns against the paired prompt and interprets risk/effects lists and causal understanding semantically. Before a negative text grade becomes a probe or penalty, one independent medium-reasoning audit rechecks learner evidence without inheriting the first tutor verdict. It shares the two-call main-assessment limit with contract repair. Correct routine answers remain one call; controls remain local. A probe supplying the whole missing gap is omitted rather than asking for repetition.
- **Extra credit is per part and independently confirmed.** The first grading response proposes each candidate's keyed decisions and context consistency. Full awards require all parts met, turn zero, no gap, and verbatim first-answer evidence for each. A separate compact medium-reasoning confirmation sees only the original prompt/answer and proposed complete claims/parts, never the earlier verdict. Missing list members, wrong category membership and topical overlap cannot establish a complete candidate. At most one optional confirmation batch is made. Failure discards only extras, while cancellation propagates. Confirmed awards persist criterion evidence and verification provenance. Commit rechecks the exact criterion set, quotes, eligibility, current coverage and signatures. Legacy quote-only awards cannot commit; valid reviews remain atomically undoable with the primary episode.
- **Diagnostics explain the next pass.** Generation logs include criterion-support reasons, complete/missing target coverage, reviewed prompt givens and source support for scope alternatives. Session logs include semantic audits, optional confirmation requests/results and group rejection reasons with due/cooldown dates. No false request-interruption event is recorded merely because a completed screen disappears.

This adds bounded latency for negative assessments and proposed extras; it is not a blanket speed improvement. A successful build does not establish semantic accuracy or generation success. Project policy prohibits unrequested tests and manual app runs.

## Focused question quality and targeted grading (v9 / policy v19)

The v8 phone pass showed that source-correct/full-coverage questions could still be poor exercises. All five grouped questions received user flags, and two visible-control metadata mistakes caused unnecessary drafting retries. V9 preserves full-target memory protection and the independent extra-award confirmation, while changing the following policies:

- **No target-count reward.** Presentation priority scores substantive learning, a verified useful contrast/relationship, and suitable controls; it no longer multiplies by binding count. Multi-target alternatives without reviewed integration benefit are deprioritized and aren't packed into new sessions. Focused questions remain the default. New combined tasks use at most two complete targets with one objective, a shared comparison axis or a supported relationship, focused workload and no answer-revealing cues. Topic proximity alone isn't a benefit. A third unrelated definition cannot be appended to a useful two-way contrast.
- **Optional formats are chosen naturally.** The conservative enrichment prefilter requires a distinctive concept-name overlap or a saved direct connection and compatible material kinds. That only nominates a candidate pair; the designer/reviewer can still decline it. Except for a suitable scalar number variant, the schema no longer forces matching just because two concepts exist. Number keys, source qualifications, evidence and full coverage retain their existing checks. No native-input quota is added.
- **Matching is independently checked.** The verification request includes public left/right sets and criterion association, withholds the proposed right-hand mappings, and asks for ALL source-valid right IDs for each left item. Each row must contain exactly the saved right ID and nothing else. Alias-equivalent left labels or overlapping descriptions fail rather than being resolved by elimination. Per-row retrieval dimensions must agree across the set: definitions with definitions, effects with effects, analogies with analogies, etc. Reviewed objective, workload, cue safety, benefit, matching matrix and dimensions are recorded. Installation requires usable quality metadata as well as complete coverage. Standard technical terms can remain; prose options should use concise natural wording rather than copy whole note passages.
- **Visible context includes controls.** `visibleTaskTexts` contains the prompt and public option labels, never keys/mappings. `visibleSnippet` canonicalizes visible case/diacritic differences. Unmatched optional given metadata is omitted and logged rather than causing a paid bank rewrite. A substantive hidden/missing response requirement still fails support/coverage/rubric verification; metadata is not a way to award unseen knowledge. Empty display answers can be derived from the already supplied criterion propositions and still undergo independent verification.
- **Minimal scope repair.** A verifier can return an exact narrowed prompt when the sole substantive defect is a source-supported scope mismatch. The next bounded attempt changes only that prompt, reuses the original keyed criteria, source IDs, input and binding statements, and reruns all verification. It skips another drafting request only when every current missing focus target has an eligible patch. It never accepts its own repair without review. Diagnostics record the proposed repair and `prompt_only_repair` use.
- **One routine grading assessment.** A negative grade alone no longer triggers a second call. More than two criteria, a latest answer over 45 words, or a continuation/clarification uses medium reasoning initially; straightforward responses use low. Explicit reconsideration and one correction of a detected contract/evidence inconsistency retain medium reasoning. Objective responses remain local. There is no unconditional `grading_semantic_audit_requested` event. This is a latency policy change, not a measured performance claim.
- **Real learner ambiguity is explicit.** Every criterion has either no ambiguous readings or exactly two plausible readings supported by learner quotes. Unclear meaning stays unresolved and may receive an open, uncued text clarification. The tutor must not silently turn “unconscious” into “unresponsive”; a narrow local guard requests correction if that reported semantic substitution recurs. It never decides correctness from that keyword. Unresolved ambiguity doesn't change memory; uncued resolved production receives at most Hard, while a confirmed wrong reading may be Again. It can't be upgraded to turn-zero credit from later clarification. Clear sibling targets retain per-target grades; source concerns and explicit disputes retain protections. When no valid clarification remains, the affected assessment is uncertain rather than a forced failure.
- **Extras require a plausible subject before AI review.** Preparation records alternative names of each binding's actual claim subject, e.g. incoming light rather than the endpoint melatonin, or SCN rather than shared hormone vocabulary. Optional candidates must name that subject in the visible primary prompt/first answer and still pass all prior relevance/eligibility checks. Older bindings fall back conservatively to concept names/aliases. This filter never grants credit; rare complete proposals still require an independent evidence confirmation and commit-time validation.

New metadata is optional in persisted JSON. No SwiftData columns, learning events, memories, due dates or historical grades are reset or rewritten. Old groups lacking quality review lose selection priority; updating the bank is required to obtain the new designs. Preparation stays on the account's selected model/reasoning (Luna / Medium for this workflow), with 16 workers.

## Separate assessment reasoning preference

Account settings (also accessible through Recall options → Model settings) expose **Assessment reasoning** beside the assessment model. It is stored per account separately from generation reasoning and defaults to Medium. An explicit choice applies to every assessment-related request, including reconsideration, correction, extra-credit verification and explanations. Automatic retains the prior adaptive Low/Medium policy. A request captures its settings at launch. The shared capability allowlist now recognizes `gpt-6.1-terra`, so generation and assessment can both send Medium explicitly. The outgoing request's actual effort is included in the existing diagnostics; selection metadata is also recorded. No bank regeneration or progress reset is required solely to change assessment reasoning.
